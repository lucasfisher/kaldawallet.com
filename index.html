How to use Kalda

Up and running in a few minutes.

Download and make your wallet

Install Kalda and open it. Pick your account type, then create a new wallet.

Already have one? Choose “I already have a recovery phrase” and type your words.

Write down your 12 words

Kalda shows you 12 words. They are the master key to every coin in your wallet.

Write them on paper, in order. Keep the paper safe. Never show it to anyone.

Get paid

On the Wallet tab, open a coin and tap Receive. Show the QR code or copy your address. It's safe to share.

Pay someone

Open a coin and tap Send. Pick a contact, paste an address or scan a QR code. Enter the amount and confirm.

Check before you send. Payments can't be undone.

Add friends with Link Up

Standing together? Both tap Link Up on the Contacts tab. Your phones swap details over Bluetooth.

Check the six-digit codes match, then save. Far apart? Use Share my contact.

Swap coins

Open the Swap tab. Pick the coin you have, the coin you want and an amount.

You'll see what you get and the fees before you confirm. Track it on the History tab.

Want more? Read the guides. Each feature is explained at three levels: simple, detailed and in depth.

Stay safe

With a self-custody wallet, you're in charge. A few simple rules keep your money safe.

Never share your 12 words

Anyone with them can take everything. Kalda will never ask for them. Neither will anyone honest.

Keep a paper backup

If your phone is lost or broken, your words bring your money back on a new phone. Without them, nobody can.

Check the whole address

Scammers use addresses that look almost the same. Pay saved contacts when you can, and stop if you see a warning.

Payments are final

Crypto has no chargebacks. Once a payment is sent, it can't be cancelled or undone.

Questions

Does Kalda hold my money?

No. Your coins live on public blockchains. Only your phone holds the keys to them. Kalda can't see, move or freeze your money.

What if I lose my phone?

Install Kalda on a new phone and choose “I already have a recovery phrase”. Type your 12 words and your wallet comes back.

What if I lose my 12 words?

While you still have your phone, you can see them again under Profile → Backup & security. Write them down right away. If you lose both the words and the phone, nobody can get the money back.

Does it cost anything?

The app is free. Each payment has a small network fee, which goes to the network. Swaps also have fees. You always see them before you confirm.

Can I use my words in other wallets?

Yes, usually. Kalda uses the same open standard as most wallets, so your words aren't locked to Kalda.
